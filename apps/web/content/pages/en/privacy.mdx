## Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

### Types of Data Collected

#### Personal Data

While using Our Service, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify You. Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

- Name, email address, profile image, and account identifiers
- Comments, guestbook messages, and notification preferences
- Usage Data

#### Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service.

Usage Data may include Your IP address, browser information, sign-in times, and session identifiers. We use this information to secure accounts and show active sessions. We also derive an identifier from an IP address to limit anonymous blog likes.

We may use a session IP address to look up an approximate location for the active-session display. This requires sending that IP address to the location lookup service.

When analytics is enabled, We use Umami to understand visits to the Website. The analytics tracker measures pageviews, referrers, browser and operating system information, device types, and approximate locations without using analytics cookies.

#### Information from Third-Party Social Media Services

The Company allows You to create an account and log in to use the Service through the following Third-Party Social Media Services:

- Google
- GitHub

When You sign in with Google or GitHub, We receive account information needed for authentication, such as Your provider account identifier, name, email address, and profile image. We do not request access to Your contact list.

You may also provide a display name, profile image, comments, guestbook messages, and notification preferences through the Website. We use this information to provide the features You choose to use.

#### Tracking Technologies and Cookies

We use Cookies for authentication and language preferences, and browser storage to remember Your theme preference. When enabled, We also use the Umami analytics script to understand Website usage.

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies.** A cookie is a small file placed on Your Device. You can instruct Your browser to refuse all Cookies or to indicate when a Cookie is being sent. However, if You do not accept Cookies, You may not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you have adjusted Your browser setting so that it will refuse Cookies, our Service may use Cookies.
- **Analytics.** When enabled, Umami collects Website usage metrics without analytics cookies. It helps Us understand which pages are visited and improve the Website.

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies remain on Your personal computer or mobile device when You go offline, while Session Cookies are deleted as soon as You close Your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes set out below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**

  Type: Session or persistent Cookies, depending on the authentication flow

  Administered by: Us

  Purpose: These Cookies are essential to provide You with services available through the Website and to enable You to use some of its features. They help to authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services that You have asked for cannot be provided, and We only use these Cookies to provide You with those services.

- **Functionality Cookies**

  Type: Persistent Cookies

  Administered by: Us

  Purpose: These Cookies remember Your language preference. We also use browser storage to remember Your theme preference, so You do not need to choose it on every visit.

You can manage Cookies and browser storage in Your browser settings. Blocking authentication Cookies may prevent sign-in. For questions about these technologies, contact hello@nelsonlai.dev.
